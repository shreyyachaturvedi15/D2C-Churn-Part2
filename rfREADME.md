\# ***D2C Customer Segmentation and Retention Strategy***



\## **Project Overview**



This project focuses on customer segmentation using the RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) framework. The goal is to identify different customer groups and recommend suitable retention strategies for each segment.



\## **Files Included**



\- rfm\_segmentation.ipynb

\- segments.csv

\- retention\_strategy.md

\- manual\_review\_cases.md

\- requirements.txt



\## **Methodology**



1\. Created RFM features from customer order history.

2\. Combined RFM information with additional customer behavior signals.

3\. Segmented customers into meaningful groups.

4\. Recommended retention actions for each segment.

5\. Prioritized customer groups under a limited retention budget.

6\. Performed manual review on selected customer cases.
