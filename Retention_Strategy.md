\# ***Retention Strategy***



The customer segmentation exercise helped identify different types of customers based on their purchasing behavior and engagement levels. Instead of applying the same retention strategy to everyone, different actions can be taken for each group to improve customer retention more effectively.



\## **Champions**



These customers purchase frequently, spend more than average, and remain highly engaged with the brand.



Recommended Actions:

\- Offer loyalty rewards and exclusive benefits

\- Provide early access to new product launches

\- Invite them to participate in feedback programs



Why:

These customers already have a strong relationship with the brand. The goal is to keep them satisfied and prevent competitors from attracting them.



\---



\## **Loyal Customers**



These customers purchase regularly and show consistent engagement over time.



Recommended Actions:

\- Send personalized product recommendations

\- Run cross-sell and upsell campaigns

\- Provide loyalty point incentives



Why:

They already trust the brand and are likely to increase their spending when presented with relevant offers.



\---



\## **Potential Loyalists**



These customers have started engaging with the brand and show signs of becoming long-term customers.



Recommended Actions:

\- Welcome offers and onboarding campaigns

\- Personalized email communication

\- Small incentives for repeat purchases



Why:

A positive experience at this stage can help convert them into loyal customers.



\---



\## **At-Risk Customers**



These customers were previously active but have recently reduced their purchases or engagement.



Recommended Actions:

\- Priority retention outreach

\- Customer support follow-up

\- Personalized discounts or offers



Why:

These customers are more likely to leave. Early intervention may help rebuild engagement before they churn completely.



\---



\## **Dormant Customers**



These customers have not interacted with the brand for a long period.



Recommended Actions:

\- Reactivation campaigns

\- Limited-time offers

\- Reminder emails about products and services



Why:

Although some dormant customers may not return, targeted campaigns can help recover a portion of them at a relatively low cost.



\---



\# **Budget Prioritization**



If the retention budget is limited, the company should focus first on At-Risk Customers, as they are at risk of leaving but can still be retained. The next priority should be Potential Loyalists, as investing in them can create future loyal customers.



Champions and Loyal Customers should continue receiving loyalty benefits and personalized experiences, while Dormant Customers should receive lower-cost reactivation campaigns.



\---



\# **Final Recommendation**



The analysis shows that different customer groups have different needs and behaviors. A targeted retention strategy is likely to be more effective than offering discounts to every customer. By prioritizing high-risk and high-potential customers, the company can use its retention budget more efficiently and improve long-term customer value.
